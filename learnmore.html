WHERE NEW VOICES
BECOME POWERFUL STORIES

The Emerging Filmmakers Program is Kaleido's mentorship initiative dedicated to discovering and supporting underrepresented storytellers. This is more than just a program—it's a creative space where ideas are nurtured, skills are developed, and voices are given the platform they deserve. We believe great stories can come from anywhere. Our goal is to help bring them to life.

WHAT THIS
PROGRAM
OFFERS

CREATIVE MENTORSHIP

Work closely with experienced creatives who guide you through storytelling, directing, and visual development.

PRODUCTION SUPPORT

From planning to execution, receive hands-on support to turn your concept into a finished piece.

COLLABORATIVE
ENVIRONMENT

Connect with a growing network of filmmakers, creatives, and innovators within the Kaleido community.

SHOWCASE
OPPORTUNITIES

Selected works are featured across Kaleido platforms—giving your story visibility and recognition.

WHY IT MATTERS

There are countless stories that never get told—not because they lack meaning, but because they lack opportunity. This program exists to change that.

To open doors for creators who haven't been given one—creating access where there was none before.

To create space where originality is valued over perfection, and ideas are allowed to grow, evolve, and take shape.

To support voices that bring new perspectives into storytelling—voices that challenge, reflect, and redefine what stories can be.

To provide guidance where there is uncertainty, and support where there are limitations.

To turn potential into process, and ideas into something real.
