The Last Frame of
Summer1

SHORT FILM — 2024
Nostalgia ◦ Seaside ◦ Warm Tones ◦ Memory ◦ Coming-of-Age

Two childhood friends return to the quiet seaside town where they grew up. Through old photographs, sunsets, and memories, they rediscover moments that shaped their past and changed their future.

This piece explores the fragility of memory and the feeling of returning to something that no longer exists in the same way. The warm, sun-soaked tones reflect comfort and familiarity, while the stillness of the composition suggests distance—both emotional and temporal. It captures the quiet realization that some moments are meant to be remembered, not relived.

Echoes of the
Empty City2

DOCUMENTARY — 2024
Urban ◦ Solitude ◦ Architecture ◦ Silence ◦ Cinematic

A visual journey through the streets of a city at dawn—before the world wakes up. The film captures the raw, unfiltered beauty of urban spaces when they belong only to the light.

Through wide compositions and deliberate stillness, this piece asks what a city truly looks like without its people. Every empty corridor and sun-drenched wall becomes a character in its own right—breathing, waiting, remembering. The absence here is not loneliness, but possibility.

Before the
Lights Go Out3

BRAND FILM — 2024
Drama ◦ Night ◦ Neon ◦ Emotion ◦ Identity

A brand story told through the lens of a single evening—where identity, ambition, and vulnerability collide under the glow of city lights. A film about what we project and what we hide.

The use of neon and shadow throughout this piece was intentional—light as performance, darkness as truth. The visual language mirrors the duality at the heart of the brand: confident on the surface, deeply human underneath. Every frame was crafted to feel like a still from a film the audience wishes they'd seen.
